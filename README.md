
# Trinity

An app to evaluate student improvements that can be used by himself , his Parents , and the School administrator . That is also why it is called "Trinity"




## Project overview

    * For students

* Let students evaluate his improvements 
* It helps to save theme by directly paying the school fee into the app , rather than going to the school office
